DYON is a small app for writing down what you owe and what is owed to you, so that nothing depends on memory and nobody has to feel awkward.

## What it is
- A ledger that lives on your own device. Nothing leaves it unless you decide.
- No account is needed to start. You can export or delete everything at any time, with or without signing in.
- Plain words. There is no interest, no late fee and no shaming.

## Why local-first
Most money apps begin with a sign-up and a server. DYON begins with your phone or computer. Your entries are saved there first, and the app keeps working offline. Read more in [Your data stays on your device](/blog/your-data-stays-on-your-device).

## Optional extras
If you want the same ledger on two devices, you can switch on end-to-end encrypted sync. The server only ever holds scrambled data that it cannot read. DYON speaks English, Arabic and French, with full right-to-left layout.
