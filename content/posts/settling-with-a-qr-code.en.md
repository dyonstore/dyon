title: Settling with a QR code, in person
summary: When two people are together, a QR code can carry a request without any message being sent.
---
Sometimes the easiest way to agree is to sit together and look at the same screen.

## How it works
- One person opens a request or a settlement proposal in DYON and chooses **Show as QR code**.
- The other person scans it with the phone camera and sees the details before anything is saved.
- Adding it opens the normal form, so they can check every field.

## What stays private
The details travel inside the code itself, and nothing is uploaded. DYON never sends it for you.

## When it helps
- Settling a shared bill at the end of a trip.
- Agreeing a new date in person.
- Giving a record to someone who does not use DYON yet.
