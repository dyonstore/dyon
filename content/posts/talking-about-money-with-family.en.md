title: How to talk about money with family
summary: A few habits that make family conversations about money calmer, from the way you start to the way you end.
---
Money is close to feelings, so it helps to have a plan for the conversation.

## Choose the moment
- Pick a quiet time, not a meal and not when someone is tired.
- Say in advance what you want to talk about: "Can we spend ten minutes on the loan?"

## Keep it specific
- One topic at a time.
- Use real figures and dates. A shared record removes arguments about memory.
- Say "I" more than "you": "I would feel easier if we agreed a date."

## Listen first
Ask what is possible for them. A plan the other person can keep is better than a plan that sounds firm.

## Close it clearly
Say what you both agreed, and write it down. DYON can hold the note so that both of you remember the same thing.

## Where to read more
The US Consumer Financial Protection Bureau has practical pages on talking about money choices. They are general information, not advice for your case.
