The longest verse in the Qur'an, 2:282, begins with a simple instruction about debts.

> {ar:يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓا۟ إِذَا تَدَايَنتُم بِدَيْنٍ إِلَىٰٓ أَجَلٍ مُّسَمًّى فَٱكْتُبُوهُ} — "O you who have believed, when you contract a debt for a specified term, write it down." (Saheeh International)

## What many people take from it
- Write the amount, the date it was given and the date it should be returned.
- Do it fairly, in the presence of both sides, so there is no later doubt.
- Writing protects both people. It is not a sign of mistrust.
