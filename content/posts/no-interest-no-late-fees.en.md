title: Why DYON never charges interest or late fees
summary: DYON has no field for interest and no late fee. This is a choice about how a ledger should treat people.
---
Some apps add a charge when a payment is late. DYON does not, and there is no setting that turns it on.

## What a ledger is for
A ledger is a shared memory. It should help two people agree on what was promised, not push one of them. Adding an automatic charge changes the relationship from trust to pressure.

## What you will and will not see
- You will see the amount, the date, who owes whom and the status.
- You will not see a growing balance, a penalty or a red warning. If something needs your answer, DYON uses a calm amber note.
- Reminders are for you. DYON never sends a message to the other person for you.

## A note on belief
Many people avoid interest on loans for religious reasons, and the Qur'an speaks about it in 2:275. DYON does not give rulings. If you want one, ask a qualified scholar. This article is not a fatwa.
