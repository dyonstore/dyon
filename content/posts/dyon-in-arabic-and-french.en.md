title: Using DYON in Arabic and French
summary: DYON works in English, Arabic and French, with right-to-left layout, local digits and the right dates.
---
DYON is written for people who live between languages, so it is not an English app with translations added on.

## Three languages, one app
- English, Arabic and French are complete, including the in-app guide.
- You can switch language at any time. Your ledger does not change.

## Right-to-left done properly
In Arabic the whole layout turns around: menus, arrows, lists and numbers. Names and amounts stay readable even when they mix scripts.

## Digits and dates
Choose Western or Arabic-Indic digits. Dates use the names of the months of each language.

## This website too
Articles, news and events are published in the three languages, and each page links to its translations.
