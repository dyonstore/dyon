title: Exporting and backing up your ledger
summary: Exporting takes a minute and never needs an account. Here is a simple routine.
---
A backup is a copy you keep somewhere else. With a ledger that lives on your device, it is the best protection you have.

## How to export
- Open Settings and choose **Export everything (JSON)** for a full backup, or **Export ledger (CSV)** for a spreadsheet.
- Save the file somewhere you control, such as a cloud folder you already use or a USB drive.

## How often
Once a month is enough for most people, and after any large change.

## Restoring
Import the JSON file from the same screen. DYON checks the file before it changes anything.

## Your right, always
Export and delete never need an account, a payment or sync. This holds even if you stop using DYON.
