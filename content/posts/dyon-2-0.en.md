title: DYON 2.0 is here
summary: Version 2.0 adds optional encrypted sync, three languages, email updates you control, and this blog.
---
DYON 2.0 is a big step, and the core idea is the same: your ledger stays yours.

## What is new
- **Optional end-to-end encrypted sync.** Use the same ledger on several devices. The server cannot read it.
- **English, Arabic and French** with full right-to-left support.
- **A storage page** that explains where your data lives and how to keep it.
- **Email you control.** Subscribe to articles, product notes or events, confirm once, leave with one click.
- **This site.** Articles, news and events in three languages.

## What did not change
No account is needed to start. No interest, no late fees, no shaming. Export and delete are always free and always available.

## What comes next
We will keep adding guides and small improvements. Subscribe to hear about them.
