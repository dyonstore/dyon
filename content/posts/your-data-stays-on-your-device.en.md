title: Your data stays on your device
summary: DYON saves your ledger in your browser's own storage first. Here is what that means, and how to keep it safe.
---
DYON stores your ledger on the device you are using. It does not need an account, and it does not upload your entries by default.

## What this gives you
- It works offline.
- Nobody can read your ledger from a server, because it is not there.
- Export and delete are always in your hands.

## What to know
Browsers decide how long they keep a site's data. Some can clear it after a long time without use, and Safari is stricter than others. You can ask the browser to keep DYON's data, and you can export a backup now and then.

## Three small habits
- Add DYON to your home screen.
- Press "Keep my data" in Settings, if your browser offers it.
- Export a backup from time to time.
