title: Giving more time to someone in hardship (2:280)
summary: The Qur'an invites patience with a person in difficulty. Here is a gentle way to think about it, without rulings.
---
Life changes. Sometimes a person cannot return what they borrowed on the agreed day.

> {ar:وَإِن كَانَ ذُو عُسْرَةٍ فَنَظِرَةٌ إِلَىٰ مَيْسَرَةٍ ۚ وَأَن تَصَدَّقُوا۟ خَيْرٌ لَّكُمْ ۖ إِن كُنتُمْ تَعْلَمُونَ} — "And if someone is in hardship, then [let there be] postponement until [a time of] ease. But if you give [from your right as] charity, then it is better for you, if you only knew." (Saheeh International, 2:280)

## Ideas for everyday life
- Ask how they are before you ask about the money.
- Offer a new date, or smaller parts over a longer time.
- If you can forgive some or all of it, that is your choice and yours alone.

## In DYON
You can change the date, record a part payment, or mark the whole amount as forgiven. Nothing is charged for waiting, and nothing is sent to the other person unless you choose to send it yourself.

## An important limit
This is not a fatwa. Ask a qualified scholar about rulings.
