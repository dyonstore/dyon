title: How to ask for your money back kindly
summary: A few short, calm sentences can protect both the money and the friendship.
---
Asking for money back is hard because it feels like a verdict on the other person. It does not have to.

## Before you write
- Check your own record first. Is the amount and the date right?
- Pick a private channel and a calm time of day.

## Three short sentences that work
- Start warm: "I hope you and the family are well."
- State one fact: "I wrote down that I lent you 200 on 3 March."
- Leave room: "Could we agree a day that suits you?"

## What to avoid
- Public messages, threats, sarcasm or reminders every hour.
- Words that blame. Describe the date and the amount, not the person.

## Kind words are part of the guidance
The Qur'an says that a good word and forgiveness are better than charity followed by hurt (2:263). This is a reflection, not a ruling.
